Balanced super-app: career networking + campus social + discreet dating — equal pillars.

## Growth geography
1. BMSCE density first
2. Then nearby Bangalore colleges (RVCE, PES, etc.)

## Dating (Sparks)
- Visible but **secondary** (Explore tab, not home hero)
- **Growth phase: free** (monetize later)

## Confessions
- Secret crush + **mutual reveal only**
- Window: **12:00 AM – 3:00 AM**
- Anonymous wall optional alongside secrets

## Revenue
- Keep free for launch
- Monetize later via ads / clubs / premium
- Paywalls exist in code but Sparks unlocked in growth mode

## Chat
- Invest in DMs + voice + call ring + lounge + channels equally over time

## Profile
- **Professional vs Social modes** (what others see)

## Offline growth
- Club cores & event partnerships primary
